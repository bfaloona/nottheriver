# WF2 Place: coverage

Frame names are the labels printed above each frame in `wireframe.html`. Every frame exists at 360 px and 1280 px. All frames are views inside one page (stated in the second note at the top of the wireframe). "Legend" means the legend box under the plan (frames 1, 4 and 5) and the two views it leads to (frame 8 Trust, frame 9 Privacy and cost).

Rows that begin "Partly", "Not drawn" or "Not handled" say why.

## IV-F. Rules

| Label | Where handled |
|---|---|
| IV-F1 | Frames 1, 2 and 9 state it. The top bar after a search shows city and state, never the zip. The zip sits in its field only while the page is open (frame 2c and the "One page, several views" note); it is not stored and not put in the page address. The plan is drawn from the zip-area center. No device location or street address is asked for anywhere |
| IV-F2 | Partly. The page fetches nothing from an outside party: the plan is drawn in the browser and the font is the system's. One optional exit breaks the default: "Open in your device's map app" in frame 6. The frame names it as an outside party, says what it receives and what a map app normally already knows, and marks it as a cost the operator has to accept or remove. Frame 9 lists it under "Nowhere else" |
| IV-F3 | Named only in the statement of the exclusion or a link to it: the intro sentence and links in frame 1, the top bar link in frame 2 at 1280 px, the legend of frames 4 and 5, "Never on the plan" in frame 8, and the link rows of frames 10 and 10c. No brand of it is named; the text points to the exclusion set. No example shop uses any such name, and the words chosen for the drawing are "plan", "zone" and "circle" |
| IV-F4 | Score table plus a source and check date for each certification and concern: frame 6 (nearby), 6b (online), 6c (distance unknown, nothing found), 7 (with a concern) |
