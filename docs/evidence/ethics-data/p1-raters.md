# p1-raters: how three established raters grade companies
Status: closed

All outside facts were read 2026-11-11 (URLs given per fact). Pages were read through a fetch tool that summarizes; the two PDFs (Good On You methodology, Good Shopping Guide site terms) were read as full text. "My judgment" marks my opinion. Quotes are verbatim from the page or PDF.

## Answer first

- Only Good On You has a rating built for retailers (shops that resell other brands), but its retailer ratings are enterprise-only, not public.
- The Good Shopping Guide (GSG) and Ethical Consumer both publish free scores for shops and brands, both are UK-centered, and both forbid reuse of their ratings without written permission.
- None of the three offers a free, reusable dataset. What is reusable is their method and the outside schemes they check (see Leads).

## 1. The Good Shopping Guide (GSG)

Operated by Ethical Company Organisation Ltd (England). Source: https://thegoodshoppingguide.com/app/uploads/2026/09/2026-GSG-Site-Users-TCs-2.pdf

| Field | Finding |
|---|---|
| What is graded | Brands, with the parent company also researched ("Company Group Rating"). Retailers appear as brands in tables. Examples: Finisterre 91, Patagonia 70, Gap 50, Urban Outfitters 64, The North Face 50 (https://thegoodshoppingguide.com/subject/ethical-fashion-retailers/, read 2026-11-11). US-owned retailers and brands do appear in the fashion retailer table (Patagonia, Gap, Urban Outfitters, The North Face); no US supermarkets, department stores or hardware shops were seen. The table is UK-weighted (Next, Primark, Matalan, Boohoo). |
| Size and scope | "thousands of the biggest brands" (https://thegoodshoppingguide.com/); "over 75 different product sectors" (https://thegoodshoppingguide.com/faqs/). Sectors: Energy, Fashion, Food & Drink, Health & Beauty, Home, Money, Packaging, Pets, Technology. Country list: not found on the pages read. |
| Criteria | Three groups plus "Other", about 37 criteria, not every one applies to every sector (https://thegoodshoppingguide.com/how-we-rate/). Environment: Environmental Report, Nuclear Power, Genetic Modification, Organic, Fossil Fuels, Sustainable Materials, Palm Oil Free, Peat Free, Toxic Chemicals Policy, Eco Schemes, Eco Labels, Timber Sourcing, Energy Mix, Reusable Cup, Pollution, Environmental Destruction, Carbon Disclosure & Reduction Targets, Better Cotton Initiative, Transportation, Eco Certifications, Packaging. Animals: Animal Welfare, Vegetarian/Vegan Verified, Sustainable Fishing. People: Human Rights, Code of Conduct, Armaments, Political Donations, Fairtrade, Irresponsible Marketing, Conflict Minerals, Responsible Diamonds, Irresponsible Lending, Ethical Trading Schemes. Other: Public Record Criticisms, Mutual Status, Responsible Investment Policy, Ethical Innovator, Brand/Product Ethics. |
| Weights | Top/middle/bottom per criterion "generally count as ten, five, and zero points"; some criteria weighted differently (Human Rights "20 points" for electronics and clothing); total converted to a percentage, the GSG Score. Full weight table: not found. |
| Evidence | Own research; company reports and disclosures; "regulatory and government publications"; NGO and media criticism ("Human Rights Watch, Greenpeace, The Guardian"); certifications: Leaping Bunny, GOTS, FSC, Fairtrade, Energy Saving Trust, ISO Type I ecolabels. The terms add: "we do not independently audit, inspect or verify companies' operations." Lookback: 2 years for environmental reports, 5 years for criticisms. Site claim: "over 40,000 public record documents" scanned (https://thegoodshoppingguide.com/brand-directory/). |
| Scale and output | Score 0 to 100, column per category (Good, Acceptable, Poor, N/A), plus a green/middle/red band, a sector "Benchmark score" (70 in fashion retailers), and an optional Ethical Accreditation logo for paid-for applicants who meet the benchmark. Example row: "Patagonia, 70, No Accreditation". |
| No data | No general rule. Per criterion: no environmental report gets a bottom rating; no timber or transport information gets bottom; no organic/GM policy but no negative records gets middle; no responsible-investment policy gets bottom. Companies that merely fail to respond: not addressed. |
| Cadence and dating | Fast sectors "every year or so", slow sectors every two years, accredited members re-researched every 12 months (https://thegoodshoppingguide.com/faqs/). No last-updated date on the table read. Terms: "we do not undertake to update any rating or table at any particular frequency" (clause 2.3). |
| Who and cost | "Research team", headcount not stated. Revenue: "from licensing our Ethical Accreditation marks to companies, and may earn revenue from advertising, affiliate arrangements and paid content" (terms 3.2). Same clause: payment "does not entitle a company to a particular score." |
| Access and reuse | Tables free to read: "publish all the research on our Ethical Ratings tables free of charge" (FAQ). No API or download found. Terms 6.3: you may not "reproduce, republish, redistribute or exploit any substantial part of the Content commercially" nor "systematically extract, scrape, harvest, copy or store the Content ... including for the purpose of training or developing any machine learning or artificial intelligence system." Permitted: 6.5 "Fair quotation of short extracts with clear attribution and a link to the source page" and 6.2 personal non-commercial use. FAQ: "we encourage the publication of our Ethical Ratings tables" but asks you to get in touch first. These two statements conflict in part; ask them. |
| Worth copying (mine) | Three-level scoring per criterion (top/middle/bottom) is simple enough for one person. A sector benchmark that separates "above" from "below" without claiming precision. Showing the criterion columns beside the score. |
| Needs a staff (mine) | Scanning tens of thousands of documents; 12-month re-research; a lookback window on every criterion. |

## 2. Ethical Consumer

Not-for-profit co-operative in Manchester, rating since 1989 (https://www.ethicalconsumer.org/, https://www.ethicalconsumer.org/about-us, read 2026-11-11).

| Field | Finding |
|---|---|
| What is graded | Companies and brands, with parent and sister companies examined (https://www.ethicalconsumer.org/about-us/our-ethical-ratings). Retailer examples from the online shopping guide (updated 2025-10-01, https://www.ethicalconsumer.org/retailers/shopping-guide/ethical-online-shopping): Oxfam online 89, Shared Earth 80, John Lewis online 29, Etsy 10, eBay online shop 3. Other guides name Tesco, Boots, Amazon. US retailers: Etsy and eBay are listed and are US companies (my knowledge; the page does not state it). No US physical shops seen. UK-centered. |
| Size and scope | "ethical ratings of 40,000+ brands and products", 9 categories (Energy, Fashion & Clothing, Food & Drink, Health & Beauty, Home & Garden, Money, Retailers, Technology, Travel), about 125 shopping guides. Retailers guides rate small sets (19 online shops, 12 supermarkets, 27 bookshops). |
| Criteria (current, from Feb 2024) | Every guide: Climate, Tax Conduct, Company Ethos; most add Workers and Animals; Israel-Palestine added Oct 2025. Climate: three-scope emissions reporting, SBTi-approved targets, new fossil fuel involvement, third-party criticism. Tax: tax-haven subsidiaries, Fair Tax Mark, country-by-country reporting. Company Ethos: lobby groups, executive pay over 1 million pounds, pay ratio, arms/fossil/nuclear, non-profit structure, Living Wage certification. Workers: supplier code, supplier publication, manufacturing location risk, living wage, unions, criticism (different list under 50 million pounds turnover). Market add-ons: clothing (cotton sourcing, PFAS), food (palm oil, hen and cow welfare), home (harmful ingredients, packaging), technology (conflict minerals). Older system: about 300 subcategories in 19 areas. Weights: "not stated". |
| Evidence | "Direct information requests to companies"; Oxfam, Friends of the Earth, War on Want reports; news, public records, directories. Named schemes: Fairtrade, organic, Better Cotton Initiative, RSPO, SBTi, Fair Tax Mark, ZDHC, Leather Working Group, Pasture for Life, Living Wage certification. |
| Scale and output | Score out of 100 "usually starting at 0 and gaining marks" (old system started at 14 of 20 and deducted). Per-category scores such as eBay Climate 80/100. "Best Buy" and "avoid" lists for subscribers. |
| No data | Not found on the pages read. |
| Cadence and dating | "updated daily by our dedicated research team"; "each story affects a company's score for five years". Guides carry a last-updated date. |
| Who and cost | "a team of dedicated researchers", headcount not stated. Income "entirely from" subscriptions, consultancy for campaign groups, and "adverts from ethically vetted companies, grants and other income". |
| Access and reuse | Partly free: score tables are visible; "Best buys", "companies to avoid" and detailed ratings are "reserved for subscribers only". Price: not found (https://www.ethicalconsumer.org/subscriptions not read). No API found. Content Usage page (https://www.ethicalconsumer.org/content-usage): "No part of the Website may be used or republished in any form without prior written permission." and "This document does not constitute permission for reproduction under any circumstances." Collaboration requests need "clear, costed financial incentives." |
| Worth copying (mine) | Two evidence classes with a hard expiry (five years) so old stories stop counting. Tax and executive-pay checks are plain public-record lookups. Size-dependent criteria for small firms. |
| Needs a staff (mine) | Daily news monitoring; 300-subcategory scoring; direct information requests to companies. |

## 3. Good On You

Founded 2015 in Australia; "7,000-plus brands" (https://partnerships.goodonyou.eco/about, read 2026-11-11).

| Field | Finding |
|---|---|
| What is graded | Brands (own-label fashion and beauty), and separately multi-brand retailers and consumer services. Public directory examples: ARMEDANGELS Great, Christy Dawn Good, tentree Good, SHEIN We avoid, Nike It's a start (https://directory.goodonyou.eco/). US brands included (Christy Dawn, SHEIN, Nike). Retailers: "rated under a separate vertical" covering fashion, beauty, food, household, homewares, electronics, hardware, kids, automotive (https://resources.goodonyou.eco/help/brand-types-rated-by-good-on-you), but a search result summary says retailer ratings are published only through enterprise products; I could not confirm on a Good On You page. No named retailer rating was found. |
| Size and scope | 7,000+ brands. Directory counts by category (Tops 4,180, Bottoms 3,324 etc.). Countries: not stated; entries show a country (Germany, Canada, US, UK, Denmark). |
| Criteria | Three pillars averaged equally (https://resources.goodonyou.eco/help/published-ratings-scoring). Planet: resource use and waste, climate, chemicals, water, citizenship. People: supply chain risk, policies, assurance, living wage, collaboration, citizenship. Animals: range, traceability, policies, materials, citizenship. About 50 named issues in the 2023 methodology PDF (https://goodonyou.eco/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/GoodOnYou-RatingsMethodology-Oct23.pdf), each tagged High, Medium or Low impact; "up to 1,000 data points". Numeric weights: not published. Rules: large brands get stricter standards; small brands cannot get "We Avoid"; large-brand pillar capped at 64 after a major incident; small-brand planet floor 15. |
| Evidence | Public information only: brand and parent disclosures, certifications, "credible independent rankings", investigations by NGOs. Two named datasets: CDP Climate and Water scores; Fashion Revolution's Fashion Transparency Index. NGO triggers: International Labour Rights Forum, Clean Clothes Campaign, Greenpeace, Australian Strategic Policy Institute. Each data point must be "specific and precise" and "material". Good Measures lets brands submit data. About 80 certification and standards schemes are listed (see Leads). |
| Scale and output | Score 0 to 100; labels We Avoid (0 to 9), Not Good Enough (10 to 44), It's a Start (45 to 64), Good (65 to 84), Great (85 to 100). Shown overall plus one label per pillar. |
| No data | Penalized. A section marked "no info" scores zero for all questions in it; virtually no information gives "We Avoid" overall (https://resources.goodonyou.eco/help/rating-process). Questions that do not apply are not counted against a brand. |
| Cadence and dating | Goal: "review large brands annually and smaller brands every 18 months"; outdated ratings usually stay published with their rating date. Reviews in about two months after a credible NGO investigation. |
| Who and cost | Analysts: a primary analyst plus a second reviewer. Headcount not stated (team page shows eight people). Funding: not stated; products for business are a dashboard and API, Good Measures, an affiliate network. |
| Access and reuse | Public directory and app free to read (login rules not stated). Terms (https://goodonyou.eco/terms-conditions/): "You are expressly prohibited from using a bot or scraper to access material on this site and any subdomain." and "All material on this site including material on any subdomain is copyright Good On You or the relevant copyright holder." API exists ("Brand Listing API v3", https://resources.goodonyou.eco/help/api-developer-guide); access and price not stated, "Get started" goes to a contact form. Partner terms (https://resources.goodonyou.eco/help/usage-guidelines-and-branding-resources): "Do not modify, alter, or adapt the ratings data in any way" and "Do not disclose the data to any third party without our prior written consent." |
| Worth copying (mine) | Public-evidence-only rule with "specific and precise" test (blocks vague claims). A published list of the certification schemes they credit. Five labels from fixed score bands. Stating the rating date. Treating "no info" as a distinct outcome. |
| Needs a staff (mine) | 1,000 data points per brand; two-analyst review; annual cycles. |

## Comparison

|---|---|---|---|

| Count | "thousands" | 40,000+ brands and products | 7,000+ |
| Areas | 9 sectors, UK-centered | 9 categories, UK-centered | Fashion, beauty (retailers: more) |
| US shops | A few US brands in fashion | Etsy, eBay, Amazon | US brands; no US retailer found |
| Scale | 0 to 100, 3 bands | 0 to 100 per category | 0 to 100, 5 labels |
| Weights | Partly published | Not published | Impact tiers only |
| No data | Per criterion, often bottom | Not found | Zero ("no info") |
| Evidence | Own research, NGO, certs | Own research, requests, NGO, certs | Public only, certs, CDP, FTI |
| Access | Free to read | Partial paywall | Free directory; API on contact |
| Reuse | Commercial reuse and scraping barred; short quotes allowed | Written permission needed | Scraping barred; API licensed |
| Updated | 1 to 2 years | Daily; 5-year memory | 12 to 18 months |

## What this means for nottheriver (mine)

- No rater's scores can be copied into the site. Their method and their outside inputs can.
- The most reusable parts: Good On You's "no info" as its own state (the site's 0.5 default does this), tiered per-criterion scoring, and a five-year expiry on negative stories.
- The reusable data lives upstream, at the schemes and registries these raters check (Leads).
