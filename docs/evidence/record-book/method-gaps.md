# Record book run: method gaps

Answer: 27 places (G1 to G27) where `docs/plans/record-book-pilot.md` left a choice or was wrong, with what this run did (the most literal reading). The ones that most change results: G10 (FTC court entry often not shown, so two Walmart orders of $10 million and $3 million count as not final), G12 (ftc.gov search likely misses names with an apostrophe: Lowe's case search returned nothing), G20 (Exhibit 21 omits Wal-Mart Associates, Inc., the employer named on 18 in-window Walmart OSHA cases) and G24 (two TJX cases of $100,000 or more excluded from HomeGoods by the sibling rule). No script had to change and no chain was stopped. Run of 2026-11-15, nine chains after Costco.

Gaps G1 to G9 were written before any data was fetched; later gaps carry the time they were found.
