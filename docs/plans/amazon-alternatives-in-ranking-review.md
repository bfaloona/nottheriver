# Review: the Amazon alternatives research in ranking (fresh-eyes pass)

Reviewed 2026-09-26 against `docs/plans/amazon-alternatives-in-ranking.md`. Read-only; nothing else changed.

**Verdict: ship with changes.** Phases A to C are sound and the plan's numbers and file references are accurate. Six things need fixing before Phase B imports data; none needs a redesign.

## Vision fit

The repo states the vision in two parts: never Amazon (`README.md:3`, `about.html:29`), and rank the shops that sell the product by how they treat people and the planet, with every reason shown and disputable (`index.html:25`, `README.md:11`, `docs/decisions/0003-scoring.md:19` "ranking by practice is the reason the site exists"). A third strand, not yet a decision, is whether the site should favor independents over chains (`docs/STATUS.md:61`).

- **Phase A (grouped concerns with dates): forward.** It delivers the small-indicator idea in `docs/ideas.md:13` and closes the gap between ADR 0004's "curated rows show the date" and `src/render.ts:160-177`, which never shows one.
- **Phase B (import 54 findings and 6 certifications): forward on ranking accuracy.** Curated findings go from 4 rows to 57 (4 live, 54 research, minus Overstock's undated row under Q3), all on accepted sources with operator rulings behind them. Every retailer that moves is one that actually appears in results.
- **Phase C (measure): forward**, with the criteria fixed below.
- **Phase D (suggested shops): deferred by Q4**, so the plan does nothing for the "find who sells it" half of the vision. The evidence claim is accurate: I recounted the 60 eval60 responses (1,147 rows; Walmart 41, Best Buy 21, Costco 14, Target 13, eBay 8, Patagonia 6, Etsy 2) and both eval20 runs (same set plus Barnes & Noble). No `recommended` or `acceptable` shop has ever appeared. So the shipped effect of this plan is: chains that already appear sink; the shops the 26 list sites recommend stay invisible. That is sideways on discovery, and the plan should say so plainly in its first section rather than in a table cell.

Net: forward, but narrower than the research promised. The plan improves the reasons shown for shops the site already finds; it does not help a shopper find a better shop.
