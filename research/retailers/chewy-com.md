Chewy is an online pet retailer selling pet food, treats, supplies and prescription medications, plus pet telehealth, insurance and in-person vet care. It is a public company (NYSE: CHWY) headquartered at 7700 West Sunrise Boulevard, Plantation, Florida, per its 10-K filed 2026-03-25. The same filing says "The BCP Stockholder Parties control the direction of our business" (affiliates of the private-equity firm BC Partners) and that Chewy is a "controlled company" under NYSE rules; Wikipedia says BC Partners held about 80 percent of shares and 98 percent of voting power after the IPO. It is not on the Amazon blocklist. Whether chewy.com hosts third-party sellers, or sells through Amazon, was not stated verbatim in any page fetched this run.

List sites recommend it for pet supplies: GOBankingRates for discounts, free shipping and handling vet prescriptions, Goodgood for "donated more than $183 million" to shelters and rescues, and VStyle for prescriptions and Subscribe & Save.

Concerns. OSHA's establishment search (2016 to 2026, violations only) returned seven Chewy inspections with citations. The four where OSHA issued citations carrying a penalty are recorded above as concerns (current penalties: $10,000 at Jessup, PA, cited 2023-05-03, reduced from $16,072; $5,851 at Ocala, FL, cited 2022-01-14 after an inspection that followed a conveyor hand injury; $4,347 at Wilkes-Barre, PA, cited 2019-09-27; $6,156 at Mechanicsburg, PA, cited 2017-04-20). Three inspections ended in citations with no penalty and are noted but not scored: Louisville, KY (1872819.015, cited 2026-06-22), Reno, NV (1638321.015, cited 2023-05-09) and Wilkes-Barre, PA (1249958.015, cited 2017-09-22). NLRB case search found three unfair labor practice charges against Chewy (14-CA-291528, filed 2022-03-02 by Teamsters Local 955, closed with no disposition shown; 09-CA-258016 and 04-CA-202538, both closed after withdrawal); none records a Board action, so none is a concern. Wikipedia mentions a March 2023 DOJ matter about overlapping PetSmart and Chewy directors, citing Bloomberg; justice.gov returned HTTP 403, so it is not recorded.
