B&H Photo Video (legal name B & H Foto & Electronics Corp.) is a privately held camera and electronics retailer with a store at 420 Ninth Avenue in Manhattan and an online shop listing over 400,000 products (cameras, video, audio, computers and general electronics), per Wikipedia. It was founded by its owning family and has no parent company named in that source. It is not on the Amazon blocklist. Whether bhphotovideo.com hosts third-party sellers, or sells through Amazon, was not shown by any page fetched this run.

List sites recommend it for electronics: PCWorld for "crystal-clear inventory and shipping data", TechRadar as "a great place for cameras, non-Apple smartphones, and general electronics", and AmazonAlts alongside Newegg.

Concerns. OSHA cited B & H Foto & Electronics at its Florence, NJ warehouse twice (inspection 1552934.015: one serious citation issued 2022-02-17, current penalty $14,502, contested and decided by an administrative law judge; inspection 1369750.015: citations issued 2019-06-06, current penalty $7,085, reduced from $10,419). Both are recorded as accepted concerns. NLRB case search for "B & H Foto" returned 16 cases (13 unfair labor practice charges, 3 representation cases, all closed, mostly Region 29 Brooklyn, 2016 to 2021); the two charge dockets opened this run (29-CA-170519 and 29-CA-194605) closed on withdrawal with no Board action, and the rest were not opened, so none is recorded. Wikipedia describes a 2007 US$4.3 million settlement "in response to claims of discrimination against Hispanic workers" and a 2017 Department of Labor (OFCCP) settlement of $3.22 million, citing press coverage rather than the agencies; the dol.gov news search returned HTTP 403, so neither is recorded as a concern here. Wikipedia also says a 2021 New York sales-tax suit was dismissed.
