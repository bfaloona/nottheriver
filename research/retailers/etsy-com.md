Etsy is an online marketplace where independent sellers list handmade goods, vintage items and craft supplies; its about page calls it "the global marketplace for unique and creative goods" with 1.9 million active sellers. Wikipedia describes it as a public company (NYSE: ETSY) headquartered in Brooklyn, New York, and says it has allowed mass-manufactured items since 2013. It was a certified B Corp from 2012 until the certification lapsed in 2017 (Wikipedia), so B Corp does not count now. The Climate Label directory lists Etsy as certified, first and last certified in 2026, and its record marks Etsy as not B Corp certified. Lists recommend it for handmade and vintage goods and for its claim to offset shipping emissions; three of the names in the tally (A Stitch In Dye, Create Your Bliss, Mini Masterpiece AZ) are individual Etsy shops.

Not scored, but on record: Wikipedia says that in 2022 "a group of 5,000 sellers united to strike" after a fee increase from 5% to 6.5%; this is a seller protest, not a regulator or court finding, so it is not a concern row. Wikipedia also describes an investor suit after the 2015 IPO and recurring counterfeit and trademark disputes; no primary source was fetched for those. A Proposition 65 settlement filed with the California AG (Notice No. 2024-02373) is between a private individual and Etsy over lead warnings on art pigments; Etsy "denies the material, factual and legal allegations", and the agreement says no public enforcer was prosecuting the claim. Passes 1 to 3 kept it out as a private settlement; under the 2026-09-25 operator rule it now counts (see below).

The CourtListener agency-docket query since 2016 returned 0 dockets.
