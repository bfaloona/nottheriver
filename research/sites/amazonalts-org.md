"Amazon Alternatives for the ethical consumer" is a single static page: "Check out this curated list of marketplaces to replace shopping at Amazon. Shop these alternative sites to invest in ethically run companies, support independent artisans and makers and small businesses, and commit to ethical, sustainable consumerism." It has no byline, no date, no about or method page; the only owner signal is a footer line, "Follow our latest projects:", linking to incitementdesign.com and Incitement Design's social accounts, so the owner is recorded as Incitement Design from that footer link. It names 29 options (27 of them shops) in seven sections (food, home, clothes, beauty, books, electronics, misc), each with a one-paragraph reason about sourcing, labor, carbon or giving. It does not argue against Amazon beyond the intro line, so it is classed `ethical-affirmative`. Discovery agents disagreed on its kind: it has no search, database or method page, so it is an `article` (a one-page list), not a `tool`. All retailer links are plain, with no affiliate parameters.

## Score
- Independence (14/20): no affiliate links or sponsor labels (all 26 hrefs are bare retailer home pages), no ads seen; deducted because the owner is only implied by a footer "latest projects" link and there is no about page or disclosure of who wrote it or why.
- Evidence (4/20): every pick has a specific claim (for example Avocado "Climate Neutral Certified", The Little Market "nonprofit fair trade shop", Made Trade's seven criteria), but no claim links to a source or certifier.
- Substance (15/20): 29 named, non-Amazon options across seven product groups, each with a reason; deducted because Newegg, B&H and Micro Center share one generic reason, Rent the Runway is a rental service, Official Black Wall Street is an app/directory, and World of Books is UK-based (links its US storefront). After calibration 27 rows count as shops; Rent the Runway and Official Black Wall Street are under "Also named".
- Currency (4/15): calibrated from 2 to match greenamerica-org (4), the other undated list whose text dates it to about 2020-22 and whose links partly lapsed; no date shown; the text speaks of "carbon neutrality by 2022" and "100% plastic-free by 2025" as future pledges, and the server's Last-Modified header reads 2022-04-19. Linked sites checked by plain curl: 15 returned 200 and 5 redirected (livefashionable.com now redirects to ableclothing.com); 6 returned 403 or an error to curl (not evidence either way); none checked for what they sell now.
- Usefulness (10/15): organized by product type with jump links and direct links to each shop; deducted because it never says who ships where, and three picks (The Detox Market, B&H, Micro Center) have no link.
- No dark patterns (10/10): no Amazon or Amazon-owned links, no pop-ups, no repeated top pick.
