DoneGood was a free Chrome extension and mobile app that pointed shoppers to ethical and sustainable companies, screening them by certifications such as B Corp, fair trade and GOTS organic, per a HuffPost article of 2016-11-29 that names donegood.co as its site. That tool is no longer at the domain. In this run donegood.co served "DoneGood – Your go-to source for understanding a changing world", a climate and carbon publication whose footer reads "© 2026 Donegood Media" and whose about page calls it "a media platform focused on carbon clarity". The site says it is "not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with any similarly named brands or trademark holders", and neither the homepage nor the about page mentions an extension, an app, shopping, brands or Amazon. So nothing about the shopping tool (method, coverage, brand list, funding) could be assessed; it is scored as discontinued. `owner` is the current footer's publisher (Donegood Media), which disclaims any link to the tool; `affiliate_links` is `unknown` because the tool itself could not be seen. A web search this run did not surface a current Chrome Web Store listing for it; that is not proof it is gone from the store.
