"It's Time to Break Up with Amazon. Here's How.", bylined Kennedy Smith, published 2024-10-31 under ILSR's Independent Business initiative. ILSR describes its mission as to "dismantle concentrated power and equip changemakers to build communities with locally accountable power" (its about page states no legal form). The article argues against Amazon on seller fees ("as much as 45 percent"), worker safety, tax avoidance, fake reviews, small-business losses and the local-versus-Amazon environmental footprint, linking Reuters, NPR, The Atlantic, Publishers Weekly, Jacobin and ILSR's own research. It then gives alternatives by product type (audio/video, bicycles, books, cameras, gifts, groceries, jewelry, medicines, musical instruments, toys), mixing named independent shops and marketplaces with trade-association store locators and local-first business alliances, and asks readers to take a "Shop 1 in 5" pledge (one in five purchases from a local business). No affiliate links. `kind` is article rather than campaign: the pledge is a call to action inside a list article.
