"How to Boycott Amazon", no byline, "Page last updated: January 15, 2026". The Anti-Fascist Marketer is an anonymous site (no name, organization or copyright holder shown on the home or About page) that calls itself part of "a global movement for marketers, small business owners and individuals to boycott and divest from fascist tech companies" (X, Meta, Amazon, Google); its About page refers to the U.S. Constitution and the Biden and Trump administrations. Under "Why boycott Amazon?" it argues against Amazon on warehouse conditions, union-busting, harm to local businesses, Jeff Bezos's ties to Trump, links to ICE deportations, Ring and Alexa privacy, and discriminatory AI, linking CBS News, AP, NPR, EFF, ACLU, ILSR, Ethical Consumer and others. It then walks through each Amazon business (Books, Shopping, Prime Video, Audible, Kindle, Goodreads, IMDb, Ring/Alexa, Whole Foods/Amazon Fresh, AWS), names Amazon-owned brands to avoid (AbeBooks, Book Depository, Zappos, Woot, Whole Foods and others), and lists replacements by name with plain links. The site states "there is no preferential treatment or promotional affiliations on this site" (footer). Also named but not counted as retailers: five other Amazon-free shopping guides (BuyBlack.org, Cut Off the Spigot, Ethical Consumer, GoodGoodGood, Not-Ship), two link indexes under Prime Video (The Best Couch Potato List, tbcpl.lol; Free Media Heck Yeah, fmhy.net), and unnamed generic options (local bookstores, libraries, local businesses, farmers markets; "any other grocery store is better than Whole Foods or Amazon Fresh").
