Two pages of the U.S. Federation of Worker Cooperatives (USFWC). "Find a Worker Co-op" (https://www.usworker.coop/directory/) is "a joint project of the U.S. Federation of Worker Cooperatives and the Democracy at Work Institute", "a compilation of the fast-growing community of worker coops, other democratic workplaces, and support organizations." Its embedded directory filters by name, state and industry and showed "Displaying 1 - 20 of 483"; each entry gives the business name, city, state, industry and a type ("Worker Co-op" or "Democratic Workplace"). The page calls the directory "carefully curated" but warns of possible "unintended omissions or errors" and asks for corrections by email. "#ShopCoop" (https://www.usworker.coop/shopcoop/) invites shoppers to "Support democratic worker-owned businesses rooted in their communities" through "our list of worker co-op products and services from across the country", with a submission form for co-ops; the list itself is an Airtable embed that returned no rows to this run (logged in `research/raw/blocked-assess-F.md`). The about page describes a "100% member-elected Board of Directors", offices in Oakland, CA and Philadelphia, PA, "nearly 450 business and organizational members", and funding from "Annual membership dues, grants, consulting and program contracts, and individual contributions." Neither page argues against Amazon.
