"23 Incredible Amazon Alternatives for Online Shopping", shown as "September 23, 2020 | Kathryn Kellogg" on the zero-waste blog Going Zero Waste (footer: "© 2026 Going Zero Waste"). No update date is shown, though the page metadata carries a dateModified of 2025-02-24 and the text cites "a study in 2023", so it has been edited since 2020. A "why we need an alternative to amazon" section links reporting (WSJ, Green America, CNBC, The Intercept, The Atlantic, USA Today) on warehouse conditions, Amazon paying "NO US federal income tax" for 2018, the $4.6 billion Virginia HQ2 subsidy and the defeated Seattle business tax. An FAQ entry ("Kathryn, you still have links to amazon on your blog…") says other posts still link to Amazon and that the author is swapping them out. The list is grouped by product type with a reason per shop, often a certification. 18 of the 23 links are affiliate (fave.co, tidd.ly, AvantLink) or referral links, and EcoRoots comes with a personal code ("Kathryn10"), but the article itself carries no disclosure; a site-wide Disclosure Policy, linked only in the footer, says "Some of my links are affiliate links".
