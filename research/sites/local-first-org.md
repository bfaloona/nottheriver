"What's in it for Amazon?", bylined "Office", dated 2024-10-18, part of Local First's "Act Local" initiative. Local First Foundation is a 501c3 nonprofit in Durango, Colorado, bringing together "220+ local businesses and nonprofit organizations" in La Plata County and southwest Colorado, funded at least in part by membership dues. The page argues that local spending "multiplies the reinvestment back into our community, creates new jobs, increases our tax base", while Amazon's dominance erodes jobs, competition and tax revenue, citing an ILSR report (2016), a Good Jobs First report on subsidies to Amazon's distribution network (2016) and AMIBA's "All About Amazon" collection; it also warns that Amazon Smile was a sales commission program, not charity. It names no retailer: its only alternative is "Shop Local Everywhere" and a link to Local First's own member directory, which covers one region of Colorado.
