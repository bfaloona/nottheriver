Amazon Brand Detector is a desktop browser extension (Firefox and Chrome) from The Markup, introduced in "Introducing Amazon Brand Detector" by Leon Yin, published 2021-11-29 as part of the "Amazon's Advantage" investigation. While you browse Amazon, it "reveals Amazon brand and exclusive products" by highlighting them in orange, on Amazon's US site and ten other countries' sites. It identifies products four ways: Amazon's "our brands" search filter, a list of Amazon products built during the investigation, text matching on phrases such as "Amazon brand" and "Featured from our brands", and Amazon's own best-selling devices. The methodology article ("How We Analyzed Amazon's Treatment of Its 'Brands' in Search Results", 2021-10-14, Leon Yin and Adrianne Jeffries) describes a dataset of 137,428 products, notes it is incomplete against the roughly 158,000 own-brand products Amazon reported to Congress in 2019, and publishes the data on GitHub. The Markup is a nonprofit newsroom that became part of CalMatters in 2024, per its about page; the extension "does not collect any data". It tells you which products on Amazon are Amazon's own; it does not point to other shops.

- Evidence (16/20): method is published in a separate methodology article with the dataset on GitHub, and its limits are stated (137,428 of about 158,000 products); deducted because the list relies on Amazon's own labels and filters and is admitted to be incomplete.
- Substance (3/20): names no non-Amazon retailers or alternative products; it only flags Amazon's own brands.
- Currency (2/15): published 2021-11-29 with no update shown; the Firefox add-on listing shows version 0.0.9, last updated 2021-12-14, 545 users; the Chrome Web Store listing rendered no extension details to WebFetch (inconclusive).
- Usefulness (5/15): quick to install and works inside Amazon's pages, but desktop only, keeps the shopper on Amazon, and offers nowhere else to buy.
- No dark patterns (10/10): no bait, walls or affiliate links; its links to Amazon are the pages it analyzes, not alternatives.
