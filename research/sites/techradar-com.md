"Best Amazon alternatives: how to shop outside of the Bezos empire", bylined Henry St Leger (described as a freelance journalist and former News & Features Editor), published 12 December 2020 with no later update shown (the page's `dateModified` equals its publish date). TechRadar's footer says it "is part of Future US Inc"; the page was served as the US edition and gives separate US and UK lists. The intro argues there is "a hidden cost to Amazon's aggressively competitive pricing – and that's the rest of the retail ecosystem", since Amazon's scale allows prices "that would simply bankrupt smaller businesses", then offers alternatives for tech, books and crafts, and closes with a section on companies Amazon owns (Whole Foods, Audible, ComiXology, Twitch, Ring). The disclosure "When you purchase through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission" sits directly under the headline and links to the about page, which says "No outside party determines the products we cover" and that paid-for editorial content is labelled. The page itself says "we post about Amazon deals aplenty", and its "You may like" and "What to read next" boxes inside the article link to Prime Day deal roundups.
