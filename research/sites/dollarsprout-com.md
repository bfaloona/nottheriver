"14 Great Alternatives to Amazon for Shopping Online", bylined Amy Beardsley, "Last Updated: April 1, 2025" (no separate publish date shown). DollarSprout is a personal-finance site; its footer says "DollarSprout.com is owned by VTX Capital, LLC" and its about page says it was founded in 2015 by two college friends. The subtitle mentions shoppers leaving Amazon "for ethical or political reasons", but the article argues only on price (a Business Insider figure that Amazon home goods cost 34% more than at Walmart) and convenience. The list has two halves: 8 places to buy (Target, Overstock, AliExpress, Newegg, Barnes & Noble, BuyDig, eBid, Facebook groups and Marketplace) and 6 places to sell your things (Buyback Boss, BookScouter, Gazelle, Shopify, OfferUp, Etsy). The #5 entry also names Books-A-Million and Powell's in passing. A commission disclosure sits right under the hero image, before the article text; the "How we make money" page says partner pay "may impact what products we cover and where they appear on the site". Three of the selling picks (BookScouter, Gazelle, Shopify) use affiliate links. The closing section points readers to "free Amazon gift cards".
