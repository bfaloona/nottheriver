"8+ Amazon Alternatives for Ethical E-Shopping (2025)", "Written By Joy McConnochie | Edited & Fact Checked By Amber McDaniel", dated 6 February 2025 (the page metadata also carries a dateModified of 2025-07-11 that readers don't see). Sustainable Jungle is a sustainability review site; its about page says it was "founded in 2017 by Joy McConnochie and Lyall Mabin", rates brands on "22 criteria across four pillars", and is a member of 1% for the Planet. The article's "Why Choose Alternatives to Amazon?" section links Forbes and The Guardian on Amazon's pollution, labor practices and "anticompetitive behavior". A disclosure sits at the top, before the index: "This post contains affiliate links which means we may earn a commission if you buy something." Eight detailed picks follow, seven of them through affiliate or cloaked `/brand/` redirect links (one with a 20% code), then "Additional Amazon Alternatives We Love (A Long List)": about 40 one-line names by category, all unlinked except People Tree. The editorial guidelines page says "Content decisions are made free from external influences" and does not address affiliate links.

## Score
- Independence (12/20): disclosure near the top and founders named; deducted because 7 of the 8 detailed picks are affiliate links (four cloaked as sustainablejungle.com redirects), #2 carries a discount code, and the unpaid long list is unlinked.
- Evidence (7/20): the case against Amazon links Forbes and The Guardian; per-shop claims (1% for the Planet membership, "Better Business Bureau accredited", fair trade) name certifications or programs without linking any certifier (R8), and the long list gives no sources.
- Substance (14/20): 47 distinct names across marketplaces, groceries, fashion, home, beauty, tech and books; deducted because only 8 get real reasons, 5 are library apps or free archives rather than shops (Libby, Hoopla, Project Gutenberg, LibriVox, Internet Archive), 2 are big-tech stores (Apple Books, Google Play Books), and Storytel is described as available "in select countries". After calibration 39 rows count as shops; the five library apps and free archives, Scribd, Storytel and DoneGood (no longer a shop) are under "Also named".
- Currency (12/15): dated 2025-02-06, within two years; checked with plain curl: the Urbankissed affiliate link lands on an Awin "closedMerchant" page although urbankissed.com loads, and the Imperfect Foods, Azure Standard and other long-list sites load.
- Usefulness (9/15): index and category headings make it easy to scan, but the long list has no links and no pick says where it ships.
- No dark patterns (10/10): no Amazon or Amazon-owned links (checked for amazon, audible, abebooks, zappos, wholefoods, goodreads), headline matches the content; the discount code is scored under independence.
