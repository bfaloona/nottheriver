"10 Great Fair Trade Stores to Shop At", bylined Emily Turner, published 2019-06-25 and updated 2024-05-29, filed under "Global Poverty" on the blog of The Borgen Project, a nonprofit working on global poverty and hunger (its about page calls it a nonprofit but states no legal form or location). The article explains fair trade as a way to pay workers "a fair and livable wage" with "safe working conditions", then names ten fair-trade shops with a short paragraph each, mostly the shop's city, founding story and what it sells. The page never mentions Amazon: it is a fair-trade store list, not framed as an Amazon alternative. Most picks read as brick-and-mortar shops; the page says nothing about online ordering or shipping. It closes by pointing to "Change The World by how you Shop" (changetheworldbyhowyoushop.com) to find more fair-trade stores by zip code. No affiliate links or disclosure appear.
