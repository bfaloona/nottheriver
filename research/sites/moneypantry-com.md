"27 Best Sites Like Amazon (Some Better & Cheaper!)", bylined Lauren Bennett (described as a UK-based writer), "Last Updated & Fact-Checked: February 17, 2025", edited by the site's founder. MoneyPantry is a personal-finance and side-hustle site; its about and disclosure pages say it "is operated by Money Pantry Media LLC", based in Missouri, and is now "a one-man operation". The article does not argue against Amazon: the author says "I'm always buying stuff there" and is a Prime member, and frames the list as places to find deals. Picks 1 to 15 are places to shop; 16 to 27 are platforms for selling. A short notice under the byline ("Some links may earn me a small commission", "Contains affiliate links") is the on-page disclosure; the disclosure page says partner pay "can affect how and where their products appear on this site, including the order in which they're listed in comparison articles". Near the top sits a "Top Picks from MoneyPantry" block of five affiliate offers for survey, reward and fitness-challenge apps, none of them on the list, and Rakuten (pick #9, affiliate link) returns at the end as a "Top Pick, Recommended by MoneyPantry" box. The list includes Zappos, which is Amazon-owned, and the page links repeatedly to its own Amazon deal content (Prime review, "Amazon shopping hacks", Amazon coupons, "free Amazon gift cards").
