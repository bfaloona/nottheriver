IndieBound is "an initiative of the American Booksellers Association" (ABA), described on its about page as a "local first" shopping movement and network of independent bookstores. Its core tool is the Indie Bookstore Finder: search by ZIP code, city or address, with an optional filter by ownership identity (for example Black-, Indigenous-, LGBTQIA2S+-, veteran- or woman-owned), limited to "American Booksellers Association member stores that have elected to share ownership identity information publicly". The about page says a bookstore is listed by joining the ABA. The site also carries booksellers' lists (Indie Next, Indie Bestsellers) and argues against Amazon on two pages: "Cost of Amazon" (a 2022 Civic Economics report, "Unfulfilled", commissioned by the ABA, claiming Amazon's 2021 sales displaced 136,000 storefronts and 1.7 million retail jobs) and "Spotlight on Amazon" (sales-tax avoidance and subsidies, citing Civic Economics, ILSR, The Atlantic and Harvard Business School). For online buying, its eBooks page sends readers to Bookshop.org, Libro.fm and Kobo, and the homepage calls these an "affiliate program" and "partnership/affiliate program"; its affiliate page says that from 2023-03-01 affiliates could replace their "IndieBound.org buy button with a Bookshop.org buy button". Shortlist row 30 gave https://www.bookweb.org/indiebound, which returned 404 this run; the product was assessed at indiebound.org.

## Score
- Independence (16/20): owner named (ABA) and affiliate programs stated plainly on the homepage and eBooks page; deducted because a trade association is promoting its own member stores, and its main Amazon report was commissioned by the ABA.
- Evidence (12/20): inclusion rule stated (ABA membership; ownership identity self-reported by stores), and the Amazon pages link their reports (Civic Economics PDF, ILSR PDF, The Atlantic, HBS); deducted because stores are not rated on labor or environment, and the key figures come from ABA-commissioned research.
- Substance (12/20): a nationwide finder for independent bookstores plus three online options for books, ebooks and audiobooks; books only, and the number of stores covered is not stated on the pages fetched.
- Currency (12/15): "© Copyright IndieBound 2026", promotes Banned Books Week "October 4-10, 2026" and a 2026 Indie Bookstore Ambassador; deducted because the Amazon pages rest on 2014 to 2021 data and the ABA's bookweb.org/indiebound URL now returns 404. The three online retailers' links were not followed.
- Usefulness (11/15): search by ZIP, city or address with an ownership filter, plus clear routes to ebooks and audiobooks; deducted because it covers books only and search results were not fetched to confirm what a result shows.
- No dark patterns (10/10): no Amazon or Amazon-owned links, no pop-up walls or repeated top pick seen; account sign-in is optional.
