Buycott is a free barcode-scanning app (iOS and Android) and website run by Buycott, Inc. of San Marino, California (per its terms of service). Shoppers scan a product and see whether its maker is targeted by "campaigns" they have joined; the tagline is "Vote with your wallet and make every purchase count." Campaigns are created by users (for example "Boycott Koch Industries", "Support Union workers", "Support Fair Trade"), and the FAQ says "much of the product data is crowd-sourced." The homepage leads with a UPC lookup database ("over 642 million products") and a barcode API for developers ("sub-100ms response times"). It does not argue against Amazon: no campaign about Amazon appeared on the browse page, and it offers no list of alternative retailers. It tells you what to avoid, not where to shop instead.
