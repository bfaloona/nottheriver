"Boycott: How to Survive and Thrive Without Big Corporations", bylined Lisa Lightner, dated July 9, 2026 (published and updated), filed under "Special Needs Family Resources (Daily Life)". A Day in Our Shoes is a US special-education (IEP and 504) advocacy site founded in 2010; its disclosure page footer reads "© 2010–2026 Lisa Lightner · A Day in our Shoes, LLC". The article names about 30 companies or groups to avoid across grocery (Walmart, Kroger, Albertsons, Amazon/Whole Foods), packaged food, meat, dairy, fast fashion, banks, big tech (including Amazon, Google, Meta), streaming, oil, household goods and coffee (Starbucks, Keurig), with a one-line reason each ("crushing small businesses", "exploitative labor", "corporate greed"). It then offers substitutes by category, mostly generic kinds of shop (farmers' markets, CSAs, co-ops, butchers, credit unions, independent pharmacies) plus 21 named sellers of goods. No affiliate links or disclosure appear in the article body, and the named alternatives carry no outbound links at all. The site's disclosure page says the owner "is compensated to provide opinions on products, services, websites" and that Raptive places ads on the site.
